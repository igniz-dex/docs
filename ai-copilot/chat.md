# AI Copilot — Chat

#### Choice of AI Model <a href="#choice-of-ai-model" id="choice-of-ai-model"></a>

Copilot isn't locked to a single AI vendor. The platform can plug in models from Anthropic (Claude), OpenAI (GPT), and Google (Gemini) — pick your preferred model, or let the platform choose a sensible default. New models can be added over time without changing the trading capabilities underneath them.

#### Visible Reasoning <a href="#visible-reasoning" id="visible-reasoning"></a>

Ask Copilot to "show its thinking." For models that support it, the assistant streams its step-by-step reasoning before delivering a final answer, rather than presenting conclusions as a black box.

#### Transparent Tool Use <a href="#transparent-tool-use" id="transparent-tool-use"></a>

When Copilot looks something up — checking a balance, pulling a chart, computing an indicator — the interface shows exactly which action it took, in real time, so you always know when it's pulling live account data versus just chatting.

#### Multimodal Input <a href="#multimodal-input" id="multimodal-input"></a>

Drop in a chart screenshot, a CSV of trade data, or a strategy write-up as a file, and Copilot can read and reason over it alongside your live account context.

#### Conversation Memory <a href="#conversation-memory" id="conversation-memory"></a>

Copilot remembers prior conversations, so context carries forward — pick a conversation back up days later without re-explaining yourself. Deleting a conversation also purges its remembered context.

#### Real-Time, Streaming Answers <a href="#streaming-answers" id="streaming-answers"></a>

Responses stream back as they're generated over a live connection, so the experience feels like an instant conversation rather than a form submission.

#### Tiered Access <a href="#tiered-access" id="tiered-access"></a>

Copilot is offered across four account tiers — **Free, Basic, Pro, and Enterprise** — each unlocking higher-capability models and higher usage ceilings. See [Access & Limits](ai-copilot/access-and-limits.md) for the full tier table.
