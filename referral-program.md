# Referral Program

#### Getting Your Referral Code

Find a referral code at <https://app.igniz.xyz/en/referral>. Your referral link will be: app.igniz.xyz/join/YOURCODE

**Earnings Structure:**

* Earn from trading fees paid by users you refer
* Earnings are net of any fee discounts applied to your referees

***

Igniz reserves the right to modify referral program terms at any time to maintain program integrity and prevent abuse.
